About

Who we are

MT Consultancy is a development and business consultancy based in Addis Ababa and working globally. We support NGOs, businesses and development partners with program design, research and analysis, funding proposals, business and program models, training, and monitoring and evaluation. Led by Mohammed Tura, a certified grant writer and PRINCE2-certified project manager with an MBA in Project Management, we focus on practical work that donors trust and teams can deliver.
